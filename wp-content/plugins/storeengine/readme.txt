== Description ==

**StoreEngine — The Complete WordPress eCommerce Plugin to Build, Sell, and Run Your Entire Store**

StoreEngine is a complete WordPress ecommerce plugin and store management platform — a fast, modern, lightweight market leader alternative that lets you sell digital products, physical products, subscriptions, memberships, courses, services, and licenses from a single Gutenberg-native core.

But StoreEngine is no longer just an online store plugin. It now runs your entire commerce operation: a built-in POS terminal, inventory and multi-location stock control, suppliers and purchase orders, cost and profit tracking, returns (RMA), courier management, and a full multi-vendor marketplace — all without stacking 5+ premium plugins or paying for add-ons.

Whether you sell online, in person, or both, StoreEngine gives you everything to start, manage, and scale your store inside WordPress — securely, smoothly, and without slowing your site down.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Can I run a full eCommerce business with StoreEngine? =
Yes. StoreEngine gives you a complete WordPress ecommerce toolkit — products, cart, checkout, payments, coupons, and order management — so you can run a real store out of the box. Advanced operational features extend it into a full store-management platform as you scale.

= Is StoreEngine a good WooCommerce alternative? =
Yes. StoreEngine is a fast, lightweight WooCommerce alternative that replaces the typical 5+ plugin stack with one native core, and includes a migration tool to import your products, customers, and orders without losing data.

= Does StoreEngine support both physical and digital products? =
Absolutely. Sell physical goods with inventory, variants, and shipping, or digital products with secure signed downloads and license keys — or both from the same store.

= Does StoreEngine have a POS system? =
Yes. The built-in POS terminal lets you sell in physical stores with fast in-person checkout and online/offline inventory sync, so your retail and online sales stay in one unified system.

= Can I sell subscriptions and memberships? =
Yes. StoreEngine supports recurring billing, trials, upgrades, downgrades, and native Stripe subscriptions, plus gated memberships with one-time or recurring access — no third-party plugins needed.

= Can I build a multi-vendor marketplace? =
Yes. The multi-vendor feature lets vendors register, manage products, and sell from their own dashboard, with commission tracking and centralized admin control.

= Can I sell online courses? =
Yes. StoreEngine integrates natively with Academy LMS to handle course checkout, enrollments, bundles, and installments from a single dashboard.

= What payment methods does StoreEngine support? =
Stripe and PayPal work out of the box (PCI-DSS compliant), with Razorpay for UPI, net banking, wallets, and cards in India. Installments, multi-currency, free (zero-total) checkout, and order-pay recovery are all supported.

= Can I create product variations like size, color, or material? =
Yes. StoreEngine supports product variants with SKU tracking and stock status for each variation.

= Does StoreEngine handle EU VAT and GDPR compliance? =
Yes. Built-in EU VAT validation, sequential invoice numbers, a consent log, plus GDPR tools for data export, erasure, and order anonymization keep your store compliant.

= Will StoreEngine slow down my website? =
No. StoreEngine is built on a fast, lightweight, Gutenberg-native core designed to keep your site quick instead of loading multiple heavy plugins.

= Can I migrate from WooCommerce to StoreEngine without losing data? =
Yes. The built-in migration tool imports your products, customers, and orders accurately, without breaking your existing layout or store setup.

= Does StoreEngine work with my existing WordPress theme? =
Yes. StoreEngine's block-based, modular design works with standard WordPress themes. (eCommerce blocks require the free aBlocks plugin.)

= Is StoreEngine actively developed, and can I request a feature? =
Yes. StoreEngine ships new features continuously — recently adding POS, multi-location inventory, suppliers, RMA, and more. You can request features and follow progress on the public roadmap.
